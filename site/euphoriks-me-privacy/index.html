Privacy Policy

Euphoriks Me privacy policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026

Euphoriks Me is a local-first Android action assistant. This policy describes the current implementation of the app.

Summary

Euphoriks Me has no Euphoriks account, cloud database, backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cookies, or cloud AI processing. Commands and Promise Radar processing happen on the device.

WhatsApp notification access

Promise Radar is optional. If you enable Android Notification Access, Euphoriks Me can receive new notification snippets from WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business. A snippet can include the notification title, sender label, and visible notification text.

Android does not provide Euphoriks Me with WhatsApp chat history or the WhatsApp database. Muted chats, hidden previews, and messages that do not create a visible notification may not be available. Euphoriks Me is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Notification snippets are held locally in the Android app sandbox until you tap Sync. Promise Radar applies deterministic on-device rules to identify possible promises, requests, deadlines, payments, and appointments. Raw snippets are removed from Euphoriks Me after Sync. Nothing is uploaded to Euphoriks Me or a third-party AI service.

Review and reminders

Suggestions are shown for your review. Euphoriks Me does not automatically create an alarm or calendar event from a message. When you approve a suggestion, Android opens the device alarm or calendar application; that application may store the reminder according to its own privacy policy.

Commands and shared text

You can type or speak commands such as navigation, calls, messages, timers, alarms, calendar entries, search, email, and sharing. You can also use Android Share to send selected text to Euphoriks Me. The app passes approved actions to Android intents or the relevant installed application. Euphoriks Me does not maintain a cloud command history.

Permissions and choices

Notification Access is requested only after an in-app explanation and can be revoked in Android Settings. You can stop Promise Radar by revoking that access, clearing Euphoriks Me app storage, or uninstalling the app. Android and other applications may retain data they created after an approved action.

Data sharing and retention

Euphoriks Me does not sell, share, advertise against, or transmit message content. Temporary notification snippets remain on the phone only until Sync removes them or you clear app data. The app does not request access to WhatsApp chat history, contacts, SMS history, or call history.

Children

Euphoriks Me is a general personal productivity assistant and is not directed to children. Do not use it to monitor another person's messages without their knowledge and authorization.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Euphoriks Me adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, remote AI processing, or new data access.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact provided in the Euphoriks Me Google Play listing. The public hosting repository is Euphoriks Me.
